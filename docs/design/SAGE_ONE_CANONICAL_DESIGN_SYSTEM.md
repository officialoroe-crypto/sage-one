# SAGE ONE — Canonical Design System

Status: Canonical working specification based on recorded owner decisions. Last reviewed: 2026-10-11.

## Brand identity
- Canonical mark: metallic/energy S inside a blue sphere, orbital ring, exactly one orbiting orb/coin, SAGE ONE wordmark.
- Use the actual canonical logo asset where available; do not substitute a CSS/Flutter approximation in production.
- The brand record names SAGE_ONE_canonical_logo.png; verify its current repository location before migration. Do not fabricate a replacement if missing.
- Keep the mark consistent across splash, navigation, app, web, and marketing.

## Visual direction
- Premium, calm, futuristic, cinematic; not a generic AI template.
- Foundation: deep black and deep blue. Primary energy: electric blue, cyan, white. Purple/violet is a restrained accent; gold/white is used for achievement and earnings where specified.
- Purple and light-sky-blue references remain controlled accents, not a replacement for the dark foundation.
- Avoid clutter, excessive neon, cheap glow, random gradients, and unreadable text.
- Prioritize responsive mobile-first layouts, clear hierarchy, contrast, touch targets, and performance.

## Himalayan Evolution environment
Where this scene is used: Everest, dark sky, stars, moon, blue-white moonlight, and a golden Buddha positioned between Everest and the moon. Do not force this scene onto every screen.

## Status and motion
- Research active: blue; analysis: purple; verification: cyan; recovery: purple to light sky blue; success: green; earnings/achievement: gold; failure: red/orange.
- Motion communicates real status and respects reduced-motion settings. Never use animation to conceal a stalled task.
- Success reveals the official logo and actual result/artifact.

## Product screens
- Mobile-first and responsive desktop.
- Reusable components with real backend state.
- Every action needs loading, empty, error, retry/cancel, permission, and success behavior as relevant.
- Research shows source names and provenance.
- Marketplace/Opportunities is easy to find and covers jobs/vacancies, buy/sell, rent, land/property, and other opportunities.
- Voice-first use minimizes typing while clearly showing listening/sending state.
- Screen-aware assistance is specified in product/SCREEN_AWARE_ASSISTANT.md.

## Evolution
- 13 ranks × 3 intensity tiers = 39 states, not 36 or 37.
- Canonical names are in EVOLUTION_SYSTEM_FINAL_LOCKED.md; the older Beginner/Learner/Explorer image is historical.
- Latest owner decision: progression goes upward based on money earned. Achievements can award SAGE Spark Points (SP), not withdrawable SAGE Spark (SS).
- Spending points or money must never lower a rank.
- Before implementing a formula, define whether money earned means verified gross earnings or net settled earnings and document exact thresholds. Do not invent thresholds.

## Copy hierarchy
Website headline recorded: “Think less. Execute more.”
Brand description: “Your personal AI mentor & execution partner.”
“Your AI Operating System” and “Think • Plan • Execute • Grow” may coexist only when their placement/role is explicit.

## Asset governance
Catalogue assets under docs/design with descriptive filenames and a manifest. Inspect, hash, trace references, and label status before renaming, replacing, or deleting. Status values: APPROVED, HISTORICAL, SUPERSEDED, UNREVIEWED.

## Acceptance checklist
Link every screen to requirements and approved reference; compare mobile/desktop rendering; check logo, palette, typography, hierarchy, motion, contrast and reduced-motion; test every visible action and its failure/success states; record evidence and deviations.
