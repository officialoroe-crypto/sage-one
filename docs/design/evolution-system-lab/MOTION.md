# Evolution Motion Storyboard

## Rank transition: nine required beats

The approved reference describes this order. Preserve it as the canonical transition storyboard:

1. **Particles accelerate** — existing material particles increase speed and density.
2. **Energy concentrates** — visual energy draws toward the SAGE emblem/core.
3. **Screen darkens** — briefly reduce surrounding scene luminance to focus attention.
4. **Shockwave / burst** — one clear transition pulse radiates outward.
5. **Old material breaks** — previous material fragments separate and disperse.
6. **New material forms** — next material appears from the transition energy.
7. **New particles appear** — particles adopt the new material identity.
8. **New rank crystallizes** — the new emblem resolves sharply and becomes readable.
9. **Interface settles** — particles slow, glow stabilizes, and the screen returns to rest.

## Example transition
The concept board demonstrates **Bronze High → Silver Low**. The outgoing state should visibly release Bronze material; the incoming state should resolve as Silver Low. The permanent SAGE blue/black identity remains throughout.

## Motion principles
- Make the stage change legible; avoid continuous spectacle that obscures rank information.
- Use one dominant transition pulse, not repeated competing flashes.
- Keep the central SAGE mark readable during the transition.
- Preserve stage-specific material particles and accents.
- Provide reduced-motion behavior for users who disable motion: crossfade and material-state swap, without rapid particles or shockwave.
- Do not use color alone to communicate rank; include stage name, number and emblem.
- Timing values remain exploratory until reviewed against the original reference and implemented in a motion prototype.
