## Three intensity states (per stage)

These are three presentations of the same Evolution, not three rank steps.

### Low
- Subtle material particles.
- Lower glow and restrained highlights.
- Gentle material accents around the SAGE emblem.
- Permanent blue/black interface and identity remain clear.

### Mid
- More material particles/fragments.
- Stronger glow and more visible material detail.
- Clearer stage identity without overwhelming the SAGE emblem.

### High
- Maximum stage-specific intensity.
- Richer and denser particles.
- Stronger material energy and a pronounced stage emblem.
- Still retain the permanent SAGE blue/black identity.

## Emblem consistency
Every stage emblem should occupy the same visual role and comparable optical footprint. Stage changes alter the material treatment and detail, not the underlying SAGE identity. Do not substitute unrelated generic badges.

## Palette status
The reference explicitly states the exact 13-stage hex palette is not finalized. Use the image as the visual target during exploration; maintain a palette register with values marked **provisional** until the owner approves them. Do not label sampled/estimated values as official.
