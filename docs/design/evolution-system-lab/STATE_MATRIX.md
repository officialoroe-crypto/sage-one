# 39-State Visual Matrix

This matrix defines the three intensity treatments for each of the thirteen Evolutions. It does not create 39 ranks. Stage-specific visual descriptions are directions from the approved concept image, not final material shaders or color tokens.

## Shared state rules

| Property | Low | Mid | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Particle count | Sparse | Moderate | Dense / rich |
| Particle speed | Slow drift | Visible movement | Energetic, controlled |
| Material glow | Restrained | Clearly visible | Maximum stage intensity |
| Emblem detail | Clean silhouette | Material detail readable | Full detail and crisp facets |
| SAGE blue/black | Dominant | Dominant | Still dominant |
| Interface recolor | None | None | None |

## Stage-by-stage state direction

| # | Stage | Low | Mid | High |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bronze | Fine copper flecks; faint warm edge | More copper fragments; stronger metallic edge | Rich copper energy and dense sparks; blue/black UI retained |
| 2 | Silver | Sparse cool-white glints | Clear silver facets and brighter edge | Bright crystalline silver highlights; avoid washing out SAGE blue |
| 3 | Gold | Small gold glints | Defined gold facets and warm glow | Strong gold energy and particles; gold remains an accent |
| 4 | Platinum | Pale icy shimmer | Sharper ice-like facets | Brilliant platinum/ice reflections; preserve dark contrast |
| 5 | Jade | Tiny green inclusions | Visible jade facets and green particles | Rich jade energy and stronger material definition |
| 6 | Ruby | Restrained red glints | Ruby facets and brighter crimson accents | Dense red energy and sharp gemstone highlights |
| 7 | Sapphire | Subtle deep-blue facets | Sapphire facets become distinct from base SAGE blue | Rich sapphire crystalline detail while SAGE emblem stays identifiable |
| 8 | Emerald | Sparse green glints | Clear emerald facets and particles | Strong emerald material energy without recoloring the screen |
| 9 | Diamond Sovereign | Few white crystalline points | Bright diamond facets and defined highlights | Maximum clear/white crystalline intensity with controlled bloom |
| 10 | Black Opal Realm | Dark iridescent flashes | Visible multi-angle opalescence | Rich opalescent color shifts over a dark base; never flatten into rainbow UI |
| 11 | Painite Core | Small rare red/orange sparks | Defined red/orange mineral facets | Dense fiery mineral energy and rare orange highlights |
| 12 | Void Matter | Sparse violet/cosmic glints | Deeper violet depth and particles | Strong cosmic-violet energy while retaining blue/black SAGE foundation |
| 13 | Californium Overlord | Rare cosmic-gold points | Distinct ultra-rare luminous facets | Most elaborate cosmic material treatment; high intensity without losing emblem legibility |

## Consistency constraints
- Keep the same emblem footprint and alignment across all stages.
- Low/Mid/High must be distinguishable by particle density, glow strength and material detail, not by changing the overall UI theme.
- Keep labels and stage number readable in every state.
- Avoid clipping glows at card edges.
- Use restrained bloom so neighboring cards remain visually distinct.
- The reference board shows conceptual material accents; exact color values remain owner-approval items.
