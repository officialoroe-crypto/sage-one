# SAGE ONE Form Notice and Consent Copy — Internal Draft Only

**DRAFT — NOT FOR PUBLICATION.** A checkbox alone is not proof of lawful consent; text must be wired to real backend enforcement and records.

## Required at collection
Before a form submits, show a short notice stating:
- Which information is required and optional.
- The specific purpose for each field.
- Which service/provider receives it, where applicable.
- A link to the current Privacy Policy.
- How to access, correct, or delete it, and a verified contact for questions.

## Optional memory
Suggested wording, subject to review and connection to actual behavior:

“I agree that SAGE ONE may save the preferences and information I choose to share as memory to personalize future responses. I can review and delete saved memories in settings.”

This must be optional, unchecked by default, recorded with timestamp and policy/version, and must actually prevent memory learning when declined. Do not bundle it with necessary account processing.

## Optional marketing
If marketing messages are introduced, use a separate optional, unchecked choice. Do not require marketing consent to use the core service. Record and honor withdrawal.
